Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the duration of the programme?

A: The Shadow SDG Entrepreneurship programme is 6 weeks, with a Market Day in Week 5.

Q: Which schools do you partner with?

A: We partner with schools across Nigeria, including Jasmine School, Freedom Assembly, and Excel Academy.

Q: What is the cost?

A: Programme fee is ₦10,000 per student, plus materials cost of ₦25,000.

Q: What is SDG Entrepreneurship?

A: Building a business that serves a UN Sustainable Development Goal.
